The Power of Horary Prashna & Tarot

Frequently, devotees find themselves at urgent crossroads without an exact birth time, or faced with an immediate dilemma: "Should I accept this foreign job offer?", "Will this litigation resolve peacefully?", "Is this business venture aligned with my dharma?" Prashna Shastra calculates the exact planetary configuration at the moment the query is sincerely posed, providing direct, unfiltered cosmic insight.

What Prashna Consultation Solves

1. Career Turning Points Job switches, promotions, foreign relocation timing, and investment decisions.
2. Health & Surgical Recoveries Favorable timing for treatments, underlying psychological stress roots, and Mahamrityunjaya ahuti recommendations.
3. Relationship Crossroads Navigating sensitive partnership choices, family disputes, and matrimonial timing with spiritual dignity.
4. Lost Property & Legal Peace Ancient Daivajna principles to trace missing valuables and assess the trajectory of property disputes.
