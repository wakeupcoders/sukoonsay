Griha Shanti & Vastu Dosha Nivaran Havan

Sanctify residential and commercial spaces, clear ancestral vastu blockages, and harmonize energy fields through authentic Vedic Vastu Havan at Shri Sarveshwar Mandir.

Family Harmony & Vastu Shuddhi Typical Duration: 2 Hours
वास्तु पुरुष प्रार्थना मन्त्र

नमस्ते वास्तु पुरुषाय भूशय्याभिरताय च।
मद्गृहं धनधान्यादि समृद्धं कुरु सर्वदा॥

"Salutations to Vastu Purusha, guardian of the dwelling! Bless our home with everlasting health, peace, wealth, and abundance."

Significance of Griha Shanti & Vastu Havan

Every plot, apartment, or house absorbs subtle geometric and energetic influences known as Vastu. Inauspicious directional cuts, entrance defects, or residual negative energies can cause frequent arguments, financial leakage, and chronic illnesses. Griha Shanti and Vastu Havan performed by the priests of Shri Sarveshwar Mandir, Rupnagar (Ropar), Punjab establishes harmony with the 8 cardinal deities (Ashta Dikpalas) and invites divine prosperity.

Ritual Vidhi & Key Highlights

1. Punyaha Vachanam & Kalash Puja Consecration of Varuna Kalash with holy rivers water, mango leaves, and coconut to invite divine auspiciousness into the home.
2. Vastu Purusha Mandal Sthapana Drawing the sacred 64/81-grid Vastu Purusha mandala, invoking deities of the north-east (Ishanya) and all directions.
3. Vastu Havan with Herbal Ahutis Offering ahutis with Palasha wood, Guggul, Loban, and Desi Cow Ghee to neutralize geopathic stress and toxic vibrations.
4. Shanti Kalash Prokshanam Sprinkling consecrated Vedic holy water in all corners of the house, followed by Aarti and distribution of divine Mahaprasad.
