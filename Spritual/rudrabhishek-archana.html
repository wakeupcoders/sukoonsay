About Rudrabhishek & Archana

Rudrabhishek is acknowledged across all Hindu Puranas as the most beloved worship of Mahadeva. The continuous bathing (Dhara) of the consecrated Shiva Lingam with sacred liquids soothes the fiery energy of Rudra and rains supreme blessings, health, and karmic forgiveness upon the devotee. Performed with authentic Yajurvedic Namakam and Chamakam recitations at Shri Sarveshwar Mandir, Rupnagar (Ropar), Punjab.

The Sacred Abhishek Dravyas & Sequence

1. Pure Cow Milk (Ksheer Abhishek) Bathing with fresh desi cow milk for longevity, progeny, and purity of thoughts.
2. Curd, Ghee, Honey & Sugar (Panchamrit) Offering curd for prosperity, cow ghee for spiritual radiance, wild honey for sweet speech, and sugarcane juice for happiness.
3. Chandan, Bhasma & 108 Bilva Patra Cooling Malayagiri sandalwood paste, sacred vibhuti bhasma, and archana with 108 three-leafed bilva patras.
4. Sahasranama & Karpura Maha Aarti Chanting of Shiva Sahasranama (1008 Holy Names), followed by fragrant incense, bilvashtakam, and grand camphor aarti.
