"Salutations to our revered ancestors and foremothers! May this consecrated water, sesame, and prayer bring supreme peace to their souls."

About Pitra Tarpan & Shradh Seva

In Sanatan philosophy, remembering and offering oblations to one's ancestors (Pitrus) is the foremost of the Five Great Debts (Pancha Maha Yagyas). When ancestors remain discontented, their descendants experience Pitra Dosha—manifesting in unexplainable delays in progeny, repetitive accidents, and career roadblocks. At Shri Sarveshwar Mandir, Rupnagar (Ropar), Punjab, Pitra Tarpan is performed strictly according to Vedic Garuda Purana Shastras.

Shastric Ritual Steps

1. Deva, Rishi & Manushya Tarpan Water offerings offered via fingers to gods, enlightened sages, and cosmic progenitors for universal harmony.
2. Pitra Tarpan with Black Sesame Facing south, wearing Pavitri (kusha ring), water infused with black til is poured between the thumb and index finger (Pitru Tirtha).
3. Pinda Daan (Rice & Ghee Balls) Offering sacred pindas cooked from barley flour, cooked rice, honey, and sesame on kusha grass bed with Vedic chanting.
4. Gau Seva, Kaka Bali & Anna Daan Feeding sacred desi cows, offering crows grain (symbolic messenger of Yama), and Anna Daan to Brahmins to seal the blessing.
