05:30 AM - 12:30 PM

The morning hours are infused with the Brahma Muhurat aura, pure conch vibrations, and fresh flower garlands.

Devotional singing in the central hall

Concluding bedtime prayers and gates closure

Visitor Guidelines & Etiquette

Attire

Traditional and modest Indian attire (Kurta/Pajama, Dhoti, Saree, Salwar) is recommended for sanctum entry.

Footwear

Free and safe footwear depository is available at the North and South mandir gates.

Photography

Photography is strictly prohibited inside the inner sanctum to maintain meditative sanctity.

Prasad

Pure cow ghee Prasad packets are distributed daily at the sanctum counter after Rajbhog and Sandhya Aartis.
