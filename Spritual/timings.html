Pratah Kaal (Morning Darshan)

05:30 AM - 12:30 PM

The morning hours are infused with the Brahma Muhurat aura, pure conch vibrations, and fresh flower garlands.

05:30 AM Suprabhatam & Sanctum Gates Open
Awakening prayers and conch blowing
06:00 AM Mangala Aarti (Ghee Lamp)
First sacred flame offering of the day
08:30 AM Morning Archana & Abhishek
Panchamrit holy bath for the deities
12:00 PM Rajbhog Aarti & Prasad Bhog
Consecrated midday sweet bhog offering

Sandhya Kaal (Evening Darshan)

04:30 PM - 09:30 PM

Evening brings the grand ringing of brass bells, camphor aroma, devotional kirtan, and twilight peace.

04:30 PM Sanctum Gates Reopen
Inner doors open for evening visitors
07:00 PM Maha Sandhya Aarti
Grand celebration with bells, drums & dhoop
08:00 PM Bhajan & Keertan Satsang
Devotional singing in the central hall
09:15 PM Shayan Aarti & Night Vishram
Concluding bedtime prayers and gates closure

Visitor Guidelines & Etiquette

Attire

Traditional and modest Indian attire (Kurta/Pajama, Dhoti, Saree, Salwar) is recommended for sanctum entry.

Footwear

Free and safe footwear depository is available at the North and South mandir gates.

Photography

Photography is strictly prohibited inside the inner sanctum to maintain meditative sanctity.

Prasad

Pure cow ghee Prasad packets are distributed daily at the sanctum counter after Rajbhog and Sandhya Aartis.
