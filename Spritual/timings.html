Daily Sanctum Schedule & Rituals

Sukoon Temple sanctum gates open daily at Brahma Muhurat. Timings adapt automatically to your selected country timezone above.

OPENING 05:30 AM IST
Suprabhatam & Gates Open

Awakening Vedic hymns, deep shankhanaad (sacred conch blowing), and unlocking of sanctum gates for morning devotees.

Conch & Morning Hymns Watch Live
1ST MAHA AARTI 06:00 AM – 06:45 AM IST
Pratah Mangala Aarti

The first grand Aarti of the dawn. Offered with pure desi cow ghee lamps and camphor, invoking solar vital energy, health, and peace.

Pure Ghee Lamps Live Darshan
PUJA & ABHISHEK 08:30 AM – 09:30 AM IST
Shodashopachara Archana

16-step sacred puja, Panchamrit holy bath for the deities, flower garland alankaram, and personalized devotee sankalpa recital.

Panchamrit Holy Bath Book Puja
2ND MAHA AARTI 12:00 PM – 12:30 PM IST
Madhyahna Rajbhog Aarti

Midday feast offering prepared with organic grains, dry fruits, and mishri. Accompanied by royal dhup, conch resonance, and prasad blessing.

Sweet Prasad Bhog Live Darshan
VISHRAM (REST) 12:30 PM – 04:30 PM IST
Sanctum Vishram (Gates Closed)

The inner sanctum observes afternoon rest and silence. Outer parikrama gardens, library, and meditation courtyard remain accessible to visitors.

Sanctum Closed for Rest Plan Visit
GATES REOPEN 04:30 PM IST
Aparahna Darshan Opening

The sacred curtain opens for twilight darshan. Devotees gather for evening pradakshina, lighting earthen oil lamps along Sutlej Kinara Marg.

Sanctum Open Plan Visit
GRAND MAHA AARTI 07:00 PM – 07:45 PM IST
Maha Sandhya Aarti (Sutlej Ghat Dipam)

The most celebrated event of the day. Grand multi-tiered brass oil lamps, resonant nagadas, bells, and unison Vedic peace chanting. Broadcast live.

Live Streamed Daily Watch Live
SATSANG & KIRTAN 08:00 PM – 09:15 PM IST
Sandhya Bhajan & Satsang

Soulful kirtan with harmonium, mridangam, and spiritual discourses by visiting Acharyas on Shrimad Bhagavad Gita and Upanishads.

Soulful Kirtan Listen Live
CLOSING RITUAL 09:15 PM – 09:30 PM IST
Ratrikal Shayan Aarti

Gentle lullaby hymns (Lali), offering of perfumed water and flowers. The deities are put to rest and temple doors close until next dawn.

Gates Close at 09:30 PM Day End

Sanctum Guidelines & Code of Reverence

Help us preserve the pristine spiritual silence and meditative sanctity of Sukoon Temple.

Sacred Attire

Traditional and modest attire (Kurta/Pajama, Dhoti, Saree, Salwar) is recommended for sanctum entry.

Free Footwear Counter

Free and safe footwear depository is maintained at North and South Mandir gates by temple sevaks.

Inner Sanctum Sanctity

Mobile phones must be on silent mode. Photography inside the inner altar is restricted to preserve meditation.

Consecrated Prasad

Pure desi ghee prasad packets and holy Sutlej charanamrit are distributed to all devotees after each Maha Aarti.
